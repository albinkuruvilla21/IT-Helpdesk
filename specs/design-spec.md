Fills are the prototype's `color-mix(text 14%, card)`, computed once per scheme (a canvas app cannot mix at run time). Status and priority colours map: Open and Medium → Info; In Progress → Warning; On Hold and Low → NeutralText; Resolved → Success; Closed → Closed; High → High; Critical → Error. `Hex<Name>` texts (HexPrimary, HexAccentText, HexText, HexTextMuted, HexSurface, HexDivider, HexPrimaryTint, HexHover, HexInfo, HexWarning, HexNeutralText, HexSuccess, HexClosed, HexHigh, HexError, HexOnPrimary) carry the same values for SVG images.

**Added tokens** (not in the standard set): `ColorPrimaryTint`, `ColorPrimaryTintBorder`, `ColorAccentText` (the accent differs from the button fill in dark), `ColorSunken`, `ColorHover`, `ColorRowHover`, `ColorLine`, `ColorDot`, `ColorInfo`, `ColorWarning`, `ColorSuccess`, `ColorNeutralText`, `ColorClosed`, `ColorClosedFill`, `ColorHigh`, `ColorHighFill`: each needed by the prototype element named in the table.
